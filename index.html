We make two products. Meridian grades the US market where the data is complete. Agora is a social network for your campus.

Meridian

The US market, graded where the data is complete.

One grade, 0 to 10, on any US-listed stock or fund, with its working shown. Each metric is measured against its own sector, then ranked across the whole rated market. A name without a complete set of factors stays ungraded rather than guessed.

Agora

A social network for your campus.

Everyone on Agora has proved a US university address, and what your campus says stays with your campus. Posts, groups, student organizations and messages, one campus at a time. Free, and about two minutes to join.

The studio

Daedalus Data Labs, LLC builds software for people who want to know how a product reaches its answers. Meridian shows the data behind every grade and leaves a gap where the data is missing. Agora explains how its feed is ordered and keeps each campus to itself.
