We grade stocks and run a campus social network.

Meridian grades US-listed stocks and funds from 0 to 10 and publishes how each grade is calculated. Agora is a social network open only to people with a verified US university address, where each campus sees only its own posts.

Meridian

A 0 to 10 grade for US-listed stocks and funds.

  1. Each name is graded on six factors: valuation, growth, profitability, momentum, analyst expectations and financial health.
  2. Every metric is scored against companies in the same sector, then ranked across all rated names.
  3. A name missing any factor gets no overall grade. Meridian leaves it blank rather than estimate it.
  4. Grades are rebuilt each morning and kept, so every change has a date.

Agora

A social network for one campus at a time.

  1. Every member has verified a US university address.
  2. Posts, groups and student organizations are visible only to members of the same campus.
  3. The feed is newest first. Posts from your groups, friends and people you follow are moved up, and the Latest view shows strict time order.
  4. It is free, and joining takes about two minutes.

About

Daedalus Data Labs, LLC builds and runs Meridian and Agora. Both publish how they work: Meridian its grading method, Agora the order of its feed. The name comes from Daedalus, the craftsman of Greek myth who built the labyrinth and the wings. The mark is two wings. Each is a Greek delta (Δ), for Daedalus and Data; together they form a lambda (Λ), for Labs. The feathers run from shortest to longest, the order Ovid gives for the wings Daedalus built.
